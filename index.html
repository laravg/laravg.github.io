Lara Vázquez González

PhD in Information Technology Research (Bioinformatics) | Collaborating Researcher @ USC

📍 Santiago de Compostela (Spain)


💼 University of Santiago de Compostela (USC)


I am a collaborating researcher in bioinformatics at the University of Santiago de Compostela (USC), where I process 16S rRNA sequencing data with QIIME 2 pipelines and carry out reproducible statistical analyses in R. Previously, I worked as an AI researcher at the Galicia Supercomputing Center (CESGA), where I contributed to the development of a One Health–oriented Data Space demonstrator and developed AIRA16S, an automated inventory of human 16S rRNA sequencing projects from public databases.

I hold a PhD in Information Technology Research from the University of Santiago de Compostela (USC), funded by a Predoctoral Research Support Grant from the Xunta de Galicia.

My doctoral research was conducted in the field of bioinformatics, focusing on the application of machine learning techniques to improve and accelerate the diagnosis of microbial diseases, with a particular emphasis on periodontitis. As part of this work, I developed PrimerEvalPy, a tool for evaluating primers used in oral microbiome analysis.

Previously, I completed a Master’s degree in Bioinformatics and Biostatistics at the Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC) & University of Barcelona (UB), and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering at USC.

I am open to new opportunities in bioinformatics, data science and applied machine learning for health. Feel free to get in touch.

Skills

  • Bioinformatics: 16S rRNA amplicon analysis, microbiome and metagenomics data processing, NGS data, primer evaluation, QIIME 2, BLAST
  • Data: public biomedical databases (NCBI SRA, BioProject, GenBank, HOMD, PubMed, Europe PMC), metadata curation and validation, database design, data spaces
  • Machine learning: classification models (kNN, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, MLP), ensemble methods, feature selection, model evaluation
  • Programming: Python (Biopython, pandas, scikit-learn), R
  • Computing & tools: HPC environments, Docker, Git, reproducible pipelines
  • Languages: Spanish and Galician (native), English (C2, Cambridge)

Featured projects

  • 16SGOSeq: curated database of oral 16S rRNA gene sequences (Scientific Data, 2025)
  • EPheClass: ML pipeline for disease classification from 16S rRNA data (Frontiers in Bioinformatics, 2025)
