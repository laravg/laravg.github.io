**Problem.** Short 16S rRNA amplicons can be identical, or nearly identical, across different species. Depending on the primer pair and the gene region amplified, some oral species simply cannot be told apart, which limits the conclusions of a sequencing study.

**What I built.** A set of scripts for the in-silico analysis of 16S rRNA amplicon similarity across oral prokaryotic genomes. They extract the amplicons generated by different primer pairs and identify the species that share highly similar sequence segments.

**Stack.** Python.

**Outcome.** The analysis supported a study published in _Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology_ {% cite regueira2022_insilico_detection %}, which helps researchers choose primer pairs that keep the species of interest distinguishable.
