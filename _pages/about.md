<p align="justify">
I am a <strong>collaborating researcher</strong> in bioinformatics at the <a href="https://www.usc.gal/en">University of Santiago de Compostela (USC)</a>, where I process 16S rRNA sequencing data with QIIME 2 pipelines and carry out reproducible statistical analyses in R. Previously, I worked as an AI researcher at the <a href="https://www.cesga.es/en">Galicia Supercomputing Center (CESGA)</a>, where I contributed to the development of a One Health–oriented Data Space demonstrator and developed <a href="{{ '/projects/5_aira16s/' | relative_url }}">AIRA16S</a>, an automated inventory of human 16S rRNA sequencing projects from public databases.
</p>

<p align="justify">
I hold a <strong>PhD in Information Technology Research</strong> from the <a href="https://www.usc.gal/en">University of Santiago de Compostela (USC)</a>, funded by a Predoctoral Research Support Grant from the Xunta de Galicia.
</p>

<p align="justify">
My doctoral research was conducted in the field of <strong>bioinformatics</strong>, focusing on the application of machine learning techniques to improve and accelerate the diagnosis of microbial diseases, with a particular emphasis on periodontitis. As part of this work, I developed PrimerEvalPy, a tool for evaluating primers used in oral microbiome analysis.
</p>

<p align="justify">
Previously, I completed a <strong>Master’s degree in Bioinformatics and Biostatistics</strong> at the <a href="https://www.uoc.edu/en">Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC)</a> &amp; <a href="https://web.ub.edu/en/">University of Barcelona (UB)</a>, and a <strong>Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering</strong> at <a href="https://www.usc.gal/en">USC</a>.
</p>

<p align="justify">
I am <strong>open to new opportunities</strong> in bioinformatics, data science and applied machine learning for health. Feel free to <a href="mailto:vazquezgonzalezlara@gmail.com">get in touch</a>.
</p>

### Skills

- **Bioinformatics:** 16S rRNA amplicon analysis, microbiome and metagenomics data processing, NGS data, primer evaluation, QIIME 2, BLAST
- **Data:** public biomedical databases (NCBI SRA, BioProject, GenBank, HOMD, PubMed, Europe PMC), metadata curation and validation, database design, data spaces
- **Machine learning:** classification models (kNN, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, MLP), ensemble methods, feature selection, model evaluation
- **Programming:** Python (Biopython, pandas, scikit-learn), R
- **Computing & tools:** HPC environments, Docker, Git, reproducible pipelines
- **Languages:** Spanish and Galician (native), English (C2, Cambridge)
